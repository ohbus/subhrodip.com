# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Preserve the historical recommendation-content contract as a committed archived record.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Archived recommendation contract

The archived record SHALL retain the localized title and item fields required by the historical design.

#### Scenario: The historical record is consulted

- **WHEN** a maintainer reviews the archived recommendation change
- **THEN** the intended structure and placeholder limitation are discoverable without treating it as active work
